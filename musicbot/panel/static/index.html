Upkeep

A check-up that fixes problems by itself, scheduled restarts, and the few things that keep YouTube playing.

Check-up

Looks for problems and fixes the ones it can by itself: starts or restarts a stuck bot, updates yt-dlp, tests that YouTube plays, clears stuck voice connections and old logs. Anything only you can fix comes with what to do.

    Scheduled restart

    A restart now and then loads the newest yt-dlp and frees memory the bot built up. It doesn't change the ping.

    In shorter months, a day that doesn't exist becomes the month's last day.

    yt-dlp

    yt-dlp is what reads YouTube. YouTube changes often; when songs stop playing, an update is usually the fix.

    YouTube cookies

    Only needed when the log says Sign in to confirm you're not a bot, or for age-restricted videos. Export cookies.txt from a browser signed in to YouTube, ideally with a spare Google account.
